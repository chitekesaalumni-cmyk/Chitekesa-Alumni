Who are we

Chitekesa Alumni • Once Chitekesa • Always Chitekesa

Chitekesa Community Day Secondary School Alumni Association (hereinafter referred to as "the Association") is a Malawian-based non-profit organization comprising former students of Chitekesa CDSS who are committed to fostering unity, mutual support, and community development. Established out of a shared sense of belonging and the desire to give back, the Association aims to harness the collective strengths and resources of its alumni to promote the growth of Chitekesa CDSS, support current students, and contribute meaningfully to the socioeconomic development of the communities in which its members live and work. Guided by the motto "Together We Make the Difference," the Association envisions a brotherhood and sisterhood of alumni working in harmony to elevate one another and the school that shaped them

Name and Establishment

The name of the Alumni Association is called Chitekesa Community Day Secondary School Alumni Association, abbreviated as CHITEKESA CDSS ALUMNI ASSOCIATION.

Location of Establishment

The Association is linked to Chitekesa Community Day Secondary School. Its operations is extend to wherever members of the Alumni are based in Malawi and beyond.

Contact Address

The contact address of the Association is maintained by the Secretary General and shall be updated as necessary. All official communications are channelled through the Secretary General.

Target Group

Vision

The vision of the Association is to see a united, empowered, and impactful alumni body that drives transformation at Chitekesa CDSS and contributes to the socioeconomic development of Malawi, guided by the spirit of togetherness.

Mission Statement

The mission of the Association is "To unite Chitekesa CDSS alumni in fostering academic excellence, community development, and mutual support, while preserving and advancing the legacy of Chitekesa CDSS for generations to come."
